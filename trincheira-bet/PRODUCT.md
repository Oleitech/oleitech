## Product Purpose

**Trincheira BET** publishes statistically-reasoned football betting analysis and tracks every call honestly. Three surfaces:

- **Tips** (`index.html`) — the day's curated pre-game selections.
- **Live** (`live.html`) — in-play alerts fired by an automated scanner.
- **Resultados** (`resultados.html`) — GREEN/RED evaluation of past calls plus running bankroll.

Success is a public record that stays honest whether the week was green or red. The results surface is the product's spine, not a footnote to it.

## Positioning

Two things a generic tipster page cannot copy:

1. **A live scanner that watches matches in play** and fires Telegram alerts on defined pressure thresholds per strategy — not a morning list and silence thereafter.
2. **Published results with a real bankroll.** Every tip is graded, wins and losses both, with stakes attached. The tracking is the credibility.

## Operating Context

**Pre-game tips** are curated in conversation, not generated by a client-side scanner: research (API-Football, news, probable line-ups, injuries) → 1–3 proposed tips → human validation → write `tips/YYYY-MM-DD.json`. `index.html` loads the file for the current date; with no file it shows "Sem tips publicadas para hoje". Absences and returns are always cross-checked against the real line-up, never trusted from a search summary.

**Stake system B**, by score band: ≥85 → €10, 75–84 → €7, 65–74 → €5, below 65 → skip.

**Markets.** BTTS is the football core (cards, over 2.5 and corners were removed). Pre-game scanners for Favoritos 1X2 (€10) and Marcadores (€5) sit beside it. NBA runs mainly Over/Under totals (🏀 badge); tennis runs ATP/WTA with an absolute floor of odds ≥ 1.50 and secondary markets — games total, set winner, handicap (🎾 badge).

**Live scanning** runs in a Cloudflare Worker (`worker/index.js`, `wrangler.toml`) with per-strategy thresholds and pressure floors, a country allowlist of roughly 50 countries that keeps alerts aligned with Betclic PT's live markets, a global star list, and a lineup-edge scanner that alerts when a listed star drops out of the XI. NBA has three live strategies of its own: 1Q HEAT FADE, COMEBACK ALERT, GARBAGE TIME. Alerts go to Telegram, including overnight.

**Results** are evaluated the next day from API-Football scores plus the worker's alert archive, written to `resultados/data/YYYY-MM-DD.json`, then rolled into `index.json` and `js/bankroll.js`.

**Deploy** is rsync into `projects/oleitech/trincheira-bet/`, then commit and push; live at `https://oleitech.github.io/oleitech/trincheira-bet/`. The rsync deliberately excludes `worker/`, `relay/`, `novo_design/`, `.cache/`, `deploy.md`, the photo folders `resultados/dia-*` and the workflow files — but **does** copy `resultados/data/`, which the app needs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS reading JSON files; no framework, no build step. Data is the deploy artefact.
- Three workflow documents drive the operator loop: `tips/WORKFLOW.md`, `resultados/WORKFLOW.md`, `deploy.md`, with NBA and tennis variants under `tips/nba/` and `tips/tennis/`.
- Admin worker endpoints (watchlist and similar) authenticate with the `lineuptoken` admin token.
- **Live scanning currently runs through a local relay on the Mac mini** (`relay/`, launchd job `pt.oleitech.trincheira-relay.plist`), because API-Sports blocked the Cloudflare egress IP permanently. The scan runs locally and injects into the worker.
- The API-Football plan dropped to Free (100 requests/day) and the bot was down from roughly June to August 2026 — the worker read API-Sports’ refusal as “no live fixtures” and went quiet without erroring. The plan is restored (~7,500 requests/day) and the error-handling fix was **deployed on 2026-08-20**. The outage stays visible as a gap: the alert archive has no dates between 2026-06-06 and the relay restart.
- An unshipped redesign sits in `novo_design/`.
- Google Analytics is present (`analytics.html`).
- Portuguese (pt-PT).
- Several leagues (Eredivisie, Pro League BE, Bundesliga AT, 3. Liga DE) were blacklisted and are due to return to the workflow in August 2026.
- World Cup 2026 (Jun–Jul) needs the star list, tips workflow and worker adapted; that work is open.

## Brand Commitments

Name **Trincheira BET**. Sport badges are established vocabulary: ⚽ football, 🏀 NBA, 🎾 tennis. GREEN/RED is the fixed result vocabulary and must not be softened into anything vaguer.

## Evidence on Hand

A real, dated archive of tips and graded results in `resultados/data/`, a real bankroll history in `js/bankroll.js`, and a real live-alert archive in the worker. This is the product's entire proof.

**Never fabricate a tip, a result, a bankroll figure or a hit rate.** A single invented number destroys the only thing the site has. Where data is missing — the June–August 2026 outage, for instance — the gap is shown as a gap.

## Product Principles

1. **The record stays honest.** Losses are as visible as wins; that is the product.
2. **Reasoning travels with the pick.** A selection without its why is just noise.
3. **Live means legible in seconds.** Alerts are read mid-match on a phone under time pressure.
4. **No data, no display.** Empty states say so plainly rather than showing stale or invented content.
5. **The operator loop is the product surface.** Workflow files and the site are one system, not two.
6. **Stakes and scores are stated, never implied.** The band that produced a stake is always recoverable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal standard was established. Two real constraints stand: GREEN/RED must never be carried by colour alone, since red/green is the most common colour-vision confusion; and live surfaces are read one-handed on phones, often in poor light.
